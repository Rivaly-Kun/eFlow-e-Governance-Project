# Phase 05 — Team, Reports, Announcements & Communications

## Goal

Bring supporting collaboration and operational-intelligence workspaces into the same productivity language.

## Targets

- `src/app/features/team-management/`
- Team Supervision
- Team Intelligence
- employee operational drill-down
- `src/app/features/reports/`
- `src/app/features/announcements/`
- `src/app/features/chat-calls/`
- notification surfaces

## Team

Use Vibe tables/lists, Avatar, Labels, Tabs, Menus, Tooltip, Search/filter controls, and Dialogs.

Do not turn raw work counts into unfair performance judgments.

## Reports

Make reports operational.

Use:
- filter bars
- view tabs
- Vibe Table
- exact-row export actions
- drill-down

Charts are allowed only when they answer a real question.

Preserve existing filters, report datasets, exports, and AI brief permissions.

## Announcements

Use a clean inbox/feed pattern.

## Chat / Calls

Migrate visible UI to Vibe while preserving realtime behavior.

Do not rewrite communication services for design reasons.
