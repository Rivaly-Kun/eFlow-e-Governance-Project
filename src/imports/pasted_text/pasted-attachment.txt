This menu is the domain of the City Treasurer, Budget Officer, and Finance Team. In Philippine Local Government Units (LGUs), the biggest headache is Commission on Audit (COA) compliance—specifically, tracking down unliquidated cash advances.

We will apply the Monday.com Work OS aesthetic to transform these tedious financial chores into automated, highly visual workflows driven by our BPA engine and Blockchain ledger.

4. Financial Oversight (The Fiscal Integrity Hub)
Design Philosophy: Eradicate "Excel Hardship." Finance teams should not be doing manual data entry or chasing staff for receipts. They should be verifying system-calculated returns and monitoring macro-level burn rates.

4.1 Master Budget Execution
Focus: Tracking the macro-level flow of legislated funds (from the SP) down to actual departmental spending.

A. Expenditure vs Approved
The central ledger where the legally appropriated budget meets reality.

Page Header & Actions: Fiscal Year Execution | [Export: COA Ledger] | [Filter: Project Transform Only]

Main Content (Monday-style Financial Board):

Rows: Grouped by Department (e.g., City Engineering, Health Office).

Columns: * Appropriated Budget (Locked value, hashed to the SP Ordinance).

Actual Disbursed (Auto-summed from the Blockchain ledger).

Remaining Balance (Formula column).

Execution Velocity (A mini sparkline chart showing the speed of spending).

UX Feature: Immutable Locking. If a user tries to manually edit the "Appropriated Budget" cell, a lock icon jiggles, and a tooltip appears: "This value is cryptographically sealed by SP Resolution #402. Edits require legislative override."

B. Overspending Risk
Where the AI Predictive Analytics engine acts as a financial safeguard.

Page Header & Actions: Budget Burn-Rate Alerts | [Action: Freeze Non-Essential Funds]

Main Content (Risk Kanban):

Cards: Projects that the AI flags as likely to exceed their budget.

AI Insight Display: A card for the Eco-Park Spillway shows: Burn Rate: 120% of Expected. The AI subtext reads: "Based on the last 3 weeks of procurement data, this project will run out of funds 14 days before completion."

Widgets: An "At-Risk Capital" battery widget showing the total peso amount of city funds currently in danger of over-budgeting.

C. Underutilization Alerts
Solving the LGU problem of funds expiring and reverting to the National Treasury because they weren't spent fast enough.

Page Header & Actions: Idle Funds Radar | [Button: Nudge Dept Heads]

Main Content (Countdown Board):

Columns: Project Name, Unspent Amount, Expiration Date, Days Remaining (A pulsing countdown timer in Red if < 30 days).

BPA Automation: The Flowable/Camunda engine monitors this board. If a massive budget is 80% unspent with only 45 days left in the year, the engine automatically pushes an escalation task to the Mayor's Executive Cockpit.

4.2 Unliquidated Cash Advances
Focus: This is the direct solution to the "Excel Hardship" of tracking down receipts and loose change from field staff.

A. Outstanding Funds
The daily operational board for the Finance team to track money currently floating in the field.

Page Header & Actions: Active Field Advances | [View: Aging Report] | [Button: Batch Verify Liquidations]

Main Content (High-Density Tracking Table):

Groups: Organized by Aging: Current (< 15 Days), Overdue (15-30 Days), Critical (> 30 Days).

Columns: * Payee (Avatar & Name).

Amount Advanced (e.g., ₱15,000).

Reported Spent (Inputted by the staff via their mobile Return Portal).

Expected Return (System calculated: ₱15,000 - Reported Spent).

Status (Pills: Floating, Liquidation Submitted, Verified & Sealed).

Visual Nudge: Rows in the "Critical" group glow with a faint red border to draw the auditor's eye immediately.

B. Leader Tracking
Holding middle management accountable for their team's unliquidated funds.

Page Header & Actions: Accountability Leaderboard | [Sort: Highest Liability]

Main Content (The "Debt Dashboard"):

The Leaderboard: A visual ranking of Program Leaders and Department Heads based on the total unliquidated cash held by their subordinates.

Metric: Displays Total Floating Cash vs. On-Time Liquidation Rate.

UX Rationale: By aggregating this by leader, the Finance Head doesn't have to chase 300 individual staff members. They simply call the 3 Department Heads at the top of the "Highest Liability" list.

C. Stalled Funds Alert
The automated enforcement arm of the finance department.

Page Header & Actions: Escalations & Sanctions | [Toggle: Auto-Demand Letters]

Main Content (Enforcement Feed):

BPA Integration: This page shows the actions the BPA Engine is taking automatically based on COA rules.

Tiered Automated Actions Log:

Day 15: Log shows Viber Warning dispatched to Juan Dela Cruz.

Day 30: Log shows Demand Letter Auto-Generated and emailed to Dept Head.

Day 60: Log shows Notice of Salary Deduction triggered to HRMO.

Widgets: A pie chart showing the resolution rate of these automated nudges (e.g., "78% of staff liquidate within 2 hours of receiving the Day 15 Viber warning").
